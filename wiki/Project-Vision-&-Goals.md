# Project Vision & Goals

## Why Commutr Exists

Public transport should be accessible and affordable for everyone. But today's route planning tools often:

- Hide the true cost until you're already traveling
- Ignore accessibility needs of people with mobility challenges
- Prioritize paid advertisements over helpful local information
- Make it hard to compare route options fairly

Commutr was created to fix these problems with a simple principle: **transparency over profit**.

## Core Philosophy

### 1. Accessibility Is Not Optional

We believe everyone deserves to know if they can physically navigate a route before they start. Commutr treats accessibility information as a first-class feature, not an afterthought.

Every route shows:
- Number of stairs at each station
- Elevator availability
- Walking distances between transfers
- Platform gaps and wheelchair accessibility

### 2. Cost Transparency Builds Trust

Hidden fees and unclear pricing hurt commuters. Commutr shows complete fare breakdowns upfront:
- Base fare for each segment
- Transfer fees
- Zone charges
- Total cost before you travel

No surprises. No hidden costs.

### 3. Neutrality Over Revenue

Most platforms prioritize businesses that pay more. Commutr doesn't.

Business listings are ordered by proximity to your route only. A small cafe pays the same listing fee as a large chain and gets equal visibility. This creates a fair marketplace where location matters more than advertising budget.

### 4. Modularity Enables Growth

Commutr is built as independent, loosely-coupled components. This means:
- New features can be added without breaking existing functionality
- Different teams can work on different parts simultaneously
- The system can scale as usage grows
- Components can be tested and deployed independently

## What Commutr Is NOT

To keep the project focused, we've decided what Commutr will NOT be:

### Not a Ride-Sharing Platform

Commutr focuses exclusively on public transport. We won't add Uber, Lyft, or taxi integration. These services have different pricing models and don't align with our accessibility-first, cost-transparent philosophy.

### Not a Social Network

While we may add community features like route reviews, Commutr is not a social platform. We won't have user profiles, friend networks, or social feeds. The focus stays on route planning.

### Not a Booking Platform

Commutr helps you plan routes but doesn't sell tickets. We provide information and navigation, not payment processing for transport fares. This keeps us neutral and focused on our core mission.

### Not a General Maps Application

We're not competing with Google Maps or Apple Maps. Commutr is specialized for public transport route planning with accessibility and cost optimization. We won't add driving directions, restaurant reviews, or general points of interest.

### Not Ad-Supported

Commutr will never show advertisements. Business listings are paid but neutral (proximity-based only). We believe ads compromise our mission of transparency and fairness.

## Success Metrics

We measure success by:

1. **User Impact**
   - Number of accessible routes successfully navigated
   - Money saved by users through cost optimization
   - Positive feedback from users with accessibility needs

2. **Business Fairness**
   - Equal visibility for all businesses at same proximity
   - Small business participation rate
   - Business renewal rate (indicates value provided)

3. **Technical Quality**
   - Test coverage above 80%
   - All correctness properties passing
   - Response time under 2 seconds for route calculations

4. **Community Health**
   - Active contributors
   - Issue response time
   - Pull request merge rate
